Privacy policy

This server is operated by {{.OperatorName}}. Contact: {{.OperatorEmail}}.

It lets an AI assistant you connect read and update your Google Contacts. Every tool call reads or writes Google directly; nothing about your contacts is kept.

What is stored

  • Your Google account id and your e-mail address, with the dates your account was created and last used.
  • Your Google refresh and access tokens, encrypted.
  • Hashes of the tokens this server issues to your assistant, with their expiry. Never the tokens themselves.
  • The assistants (OAuth clients) registered with this server.
  • Sign-ins in progress, deleted after 10 minutes.

What is never stored

The following data is never stored:

  • Your contacts: names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, notes or any other field.
  • The tool inputs and outputs exchanged with your assistant.
  • Any token in clear.
  • Personal data in logs at the default log level.

Use of Google data

Google data is used only to perform the actions your assistant requests. It is not shared, sold, or used for advertising or to train models.

How to erase everything

  • Ask the operator at {{.OperatorEmail}} to disconnect you: your Google grant is revoked and every row about you is deleted.
  • Revoke access yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The server deletes your data the next time it finds the grant revoked.
  • Accounts unused for {{.InactivityDays}} days are purged automatically, and their Google grant revoked.
