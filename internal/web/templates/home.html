Google Contacts MCP

A remote MCP server that lets AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT search, read, create and enrich your Google Contacts, with your consent. Every call goes to Google directly: your contacts are never stored on this server.

The source code is open: {{.RepoURL}}.

Connect

Add a custom connector in your assistant with this URL:

{{.MCPURL}}

  • Claude: Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector.
  • ChatGPT: developer mode, Create app.

Your assistant then sends you to Google to sign in and grant access to your contacts.
